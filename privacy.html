Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

Infographic Video is a private desktop tool used by the owner to upload educational short videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information We Use

The tool uses TikTok Login to obtain authorization for uploading videos to the connected TikTok account. The tool may use TikTok access tokens for the purpose of uploading videos.

How Information Is Used

TikTok authorization information is used only to upload videos to the connected TikTok account as authorized by the account owner.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or disclose TikTok account information or authorization tokens to third parties.

Storage

Access tokens may be stored locally on the owner's device or in a secure environment controlled by the owner.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the owner of Infographic Video.
